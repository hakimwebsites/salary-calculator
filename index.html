Why Your Basic Salary Percentage Matters

In the UAE, employers split your total salary into basic salary and allowances (housing, transport, other). This split matters more than most people realize:

  • Your end-of-service gratuity is calculated only on your basic salary — a higher basic percentage means a higher gratuity payout
  • Some bank loans and credit limits are based on basic salary, not total salary
  • UAE has no personal income tax — for expats, your take-home is generally your full salary minus any company-specific deductions

This tool gives an estimate for guidance only and is not financial or legal advice. Confirm exact figures with your employer's HR or MOHRE.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's a good basic salary percentage to negotiate?

Many employees aim for 60-70% basic salary, since it's used for gratuity calculations and often for loan eligibility. A very low basic percentage (e.g. 30-40%) reduces your future gratuity significantly, even if your total package looks the same.

Do UAE employees pay income tax?

No. The UAE does not levy personal income tax on salaries for individuals, whether expat or national. Your take-home is generally your full salary minus any company-specific or pension deductions.

What is GPSSA and who pays it?

GPSSA (General Pension and Social Security Authority) is a mandatory pension contribution that applies to UAE and GCC nationals only, typically calculated as a percentage of basic salary plus certain allowances. Expats are not enrolled in GPSSA.

How does this connect to my gratuity?

Your gratuity is calculated purely on your basic salary figure. Once you know your basic salary from this tool, you can plug it directly into our Gratuity Calculator to see your end-of-service entitlement.
